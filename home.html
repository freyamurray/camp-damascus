About our Founder

Since 2010, Pastor Pete Bend's mission has been to enable young people to resist the tempation of same-sex attraction through the Word of The Lord. He founded Camp Damascus as a way to realize this mission, and has since helped thousands of lost young souls to lead healthy, heterosexual lives in the presence of God.

Pastor Bend takes his expertise in business, earned from his successful career in Silicon Valley, and applies that to his work at Camp Damascus. As a disciple of the Kingdom of the Pine, Pastor Bend's method holds Prophet Tobias Cobel's four holy tenets at its core:

Respect, Integrity, Service, Excellence

With these tenets, Pastor Bend has successfully guided thousands of young people away from the temptation of a homosexual lifestyle. None have ever reverted to their previous condition, and all now walk with Pastor Bend in the Light of the Lord.

Sample Schedule

While no two days at Camp Damascus are the same, we structure each day around deepening campers' connection with God, discussing Christian values, and having fun! This schedule is an example of how a day at Camp Damascus might look.
